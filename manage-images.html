Exterior image manager

Exterior photography is managed separately from the editorial interior presentation. Add, remove, or reorder exterior images in content/site.js under exterior:[ ].

Each entry needs a src (URL or path) and optional label. If the list is empty, the exterior section stays hidden.

exterior:[
  {src:"/assets/exterior/pool-terrace.jpg",label:"Pool terrace"},
  {src:"https://example.com/facade.jpg",label:"Garden facade"}
]
