01 · The residence

A home with presence, without excess.

Los Verdiales is conceived as a complete private world: generous entertaining spaces, calm bedroom suites, a dedicated wellness level and a close relationship with the garden.

The visual language is restrained and tactile — natural stone, crafted oak, dark bronze, linen, soft plaster and carefully selected objects. Every room is designed to feel composed rather than decorated.

03 · Private spaces

Rooms designed to slow the day down.

The primary suite pairs generous proportions with softness: pale textiles, tactile wall finishes, dark joinery and natural morning light. The adjoining bathroom and dressing areas continue the same material language.

Wellness level

A private retreat within the residence.

Indoor pool, relaxation, training and spa spaces form a dedicated wellness environment with natural stone, warm light and a calm resort atmosphere.

04 · Leisure

Made for long evenings.

A separate lounge brings together a bar, curated wine display, billiards and relaxed seating. It is intentionally more tactile and characterful than the main living spaces, while remaining part of the same house.
