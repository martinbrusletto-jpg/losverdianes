The residence

Designed to be felt
before it is understood.

Los Verdiales is conceived as a complete private residence where architecture, interiors and landscape move as one. Generous rooms open towards the garden, while natural stone, oak, bronze and linen bring warmth and permanence.

The house is deliberately calm. The luxury is in proportion, craftsmanship, daylight and the way every space connects to the next.
