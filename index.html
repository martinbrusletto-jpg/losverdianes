The residence

Designed to feel collected over time, not designed in a day.

Los Verdiales is a house of atmosphere. Sunlight moves through generous living spaces, natural stone and crafted joinery, while the garden remains present throughout the interior.

The result is warm, architectural and deliberately quiet — a home with the ease of Marbella and the weight of a truly considered private residence.
